## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the adrenaline, discipline, and architectural clarity of high-performance boutique fitness. Bridging luxury wellness aesthetics with the raw kinetic intensity of functional athletic training, the interface communicates momentum, precision, and uncompromised standard. 

The aesthetic is built on high-contrast minimalism punctuated by explosive athletic accents. Clean architectural whitespace and structured warm-neutral paneling (#FAFAFA, #FFFFFF, #F3F4F6) create a luminous canvas, allowing deep carbon slate typography (#111827) and high-voltage neon orange accents (#FF5500) to command immediate focus. The visual rhythm relies on chiseled geometric structures, angled performance badges, clean micro-borders, and tactile interaction states that mimic industrial gym equipment and athlete dashboard telemetry.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical athletic light tones with visceral, high-chroma energy:

- **Primary (`#FF5500`):** Kinetic Blaze. Used deliberately for primary calls-to-action, key performance highlights, active state indicators, and conversion touchpoints.
- **Secondary (`#111827`):** Carbon Slate. An ultra-dense slate used for punchy headlines, dark card variations, heavy badges, and grounded structural frames.
- **Tertiary (`#FF8A3D`):** Amber Core. A supportive energetic gradient step and secondary hover/focus flare for radiant visual depth.
- **Neutral (`#6B7280`):** Iron Muted. Regulates subheadings, inactive tabs, secondary data specs, and subtle system borders.

### Surface Tokens & Color Strategy
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAFAFA` (Atmospheric off-white canvas preventing harsh eye fatigue).
- **Surface Layer 1:** `#FFFFFF` (Crisp elevated container fill, card bodies, and interactive tiles).
- **Surface Layer 2:** `#F3F4F6` (Denser utility backdrop, metric strips, badge backdrops, and inner tables).
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#E5E7EB` for soft structure; `#111827` at 10% opacity for athletic edge definition.
- **Inverse Canvas:** Dark sections utilize `#111827` as the base canvas with `#1F2937` container elevations and `#FF5500` accent glows.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the industrial, mechanical agility of **Space Grotesk** with the ergonomic legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Engineered, modern, and commanding. Major marketing headlines favor condensed uppercase or tight-tracking sentence case to convey momentum, power, and physical rigor.
- **Body & Paragraphs (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances high athletic tension with warm, approachable clarity. Highly legible in training breakdowns, schedules, nutrition tables, and class descriptions.
- **Labels, Metrics, & Badges (Space Grotesk):** Rendered with uppercase styling, semi-bold to bold weights, and generous letter tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to mimic sportswear tags, digital split-timers, and gym telemetry gear.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column fluid grid system that flexes seamlessly from desktop down to mobile viewports.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) lateral margins with an inner max-width container cap of 1280px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, 32px (`2rem`) margins. Multi-column cards compress from 3-4 items down to 2 items per row.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Side-by-side elements collapse into vertical stacks or horizontally swipeable kinetic cards.

Vertical page flow utilizes rhythm blocks of 80px to 120px between major workout modules, keeping the layout athletic, open, and fast-paced without feeling cluttered.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes crisp tactile precision over blurred shadows, leaning on structural borders, subtle warm glows, and surface stratification:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat `#FAFAFA` with no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface framed with a crisp `1px solid #E5E7EB` border. Shadows are ultra-diffuse: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Interactive Cards):** Border elevates to `1px solid #D1D5DB` or `#FF5500`, paired with an energized soft shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Athletic Glow & Floating Nav):** Translucent backdrop blur (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `16px` blur filter) combined with a faint neon rim: `0 8px 30px rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` paired with deep atmospheric backdrop scrim (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.6)`) and a tight deep shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies on controlled, ergonomic curvature. UI components maintain an athletic `0.5rem` (`8px`) base radius, communicating aerodynamic speed while preserving structural integrity.

- **Micro Components (Tags, Badges, Chips):** `6px` to `8px` roundedness for clean alignment alongside sharp monospace numerals.
- **Buttons & Form Fields:** Standardized at `8px` (`roundedness: 2`) for a confident, solid tap target. Full pill radii (`9999px`) are reserved strictly for floating floating tags or athlete status pills.
- **Media Tiles & Workout Containers:** `12px` to `16px` (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) to frame athlete photography and dynamic video feeds with high precision.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic Button:** High-visibility `#FF5500` fill, white `#FFFFFF` bold uppercase typography (Space Grotesk), `8px` corner radius. Interactive state transitions with a slight upward lift (`translateY(-1px)`) and an orange ambient shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.35)`). Active state scales to `0.98`.
- **Secondary Slate Button:** `#111827` fill, `#FFFFFF` text. Delivers heavy contrast next to primary buttons for actions like "View Schedule" or "Compare Passes".
- **Outline Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #111827` (or `#E5E7EB` in light clusters), `#111827` text. On hover, fills solid `#111827` with white text.

### Badges & Athletic Chips
- **Category Chips:** Uppercase `label-sm` tracking with subtle `1px solid #E5E7EB` on `#F3F4F6`. Selected state switches to `#111827` fill with white text.
- **Live Status / High-Intensity Badges:** Tinted background (`rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.1)`) with `#FF5500` text and a pulsing circular beacon dot.

### Cards & Workout Tiles
- **Light Tier (Standard):** White background, `1px solid #E5E7EB`, `16px` corner radius, inner padding of `24px`. Top border can feature an optional `3px` accent strip in `#FF5500` on highlighted items (e.g., "Most Popular Pass").
- **Dark Tier (High-Performance Module):** `#111827` background, `#FFFFFF` typography, `#9CA3AF` body copy, and neon `#FF5500` indicators to break page monotony and inject adrenaline.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1.5px solid #E5E7EB`, `8px` radius, and dark slate placeholder `#9CA3AF`. Focus state triggers `1.5px solid #FF5500` accompanied by a subtle orange focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Chiseled square and circular controls with bold `2px` slate borders. Active check switches to solid `#FF5500` with clean white SVG ticks.

### Performance Data Displays
- High-contrast numerical counters leveraging large Space Grotesk numerals (`40px+`) paired with uppercase muted micro-labels below (e.g., "BPM", "CAL BURNT", "SPOTS LEFT").
